Project Overview
================

Our project aims to solve the issue of localized, rapid delivery between
individuals and businesses. While services such as Amazon rely on global
networks of connected consumers, the problem we chose to focus on is scoped
much more locally; we hope to present a viable option for intracity package
shipments that is faster than services like USPS or Fed X.

In effect, what we hope to deliver is a system where individuals or businesses
can send a package within a few miles very quickly.

We believe that autonomous vehicles suit this task very well because they can
complete fast, safe, and reliable trips between different places, and the lack
of a human reduces the risk of error as well as protects the safety and privacy
of any packages shipped.

Our Design
----------

What we have decided to build for our semester project is an autonomous,
line-following rover that is capable of identifying and delivering a package
based on visual markers.

See the section :doc:`/technical_documentation/index` to read more about the
technical details of our project.
